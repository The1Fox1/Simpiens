# Epic 11 plan 1: Craft (S0–S3)

Working document (AGENTS.md, Documentation Protocol 6), one of Epic 11's plans ([index](epic11_plan.md)). Written 2026-11-16 at the producer's "Prepare to work on Epic 11 ... have the first one drafted and ready to start". The design is the epic, [`docs/epics/epic-11.md`](../docs/epics/epic-11.md) (§2.3's rules A1–A10 bind every step). Its questions Q1–Q16 stand by exception. The decisions below (D1–D12) are this plan's defaults; each moves to the epic's §0 when its step lands.

- **Complexity:** Very Complex as a whole: four milestones across the core, cognition, the save, the economy and the client. Split below so that every step is Basic or Possibly Complex. S1 and S3 are Very Complex as phases only by bundling, and no step here needs supervision.
- **Branch:** `feat/epic11-craft`, cut from `main` at `30c931b`. One PR at the end, with a commit per step.
- **Estimate:** 11–15 agent days (S0 2–3, S1 3–4, S2 2–3, S3 4–5).
- **The producer's stops:**
  - the go, before S0;
  - the look at S1 (passions and their flames);
  - S2's soak bands, by exception;
  - the merge.
- **The DoDs:** `PassionBurnsBright` (S1), `SpecialistsEmerge` (S2) and `AMasterTeachesAnApprentice` (S3), all live on the real outpost with nothing injected.

## 0. What Epic 10 hands over (checked at S0.1)
- **Skills (K9):**
  - **The data:** seven skills in `SkillTable` (capacity 16, ids never reused). A crawler's `SkillSet` is 16 experiences (32 bytes), drawn at spawn on `SimRandomSalt.Skills` (16) and saved in `CrawlerRecord`.
  - **The rules (`SkillRules`):** levels 0–10, each level costing 100 times its number. The bands are Novice 0–2, Apprentice 3–5, Journeyman 6–8 and Master 9–10.
  - **Practice per act:** a hit 10, a shot 8, a unit harvested 10, a make 60, a rescue 100.
- **The seam (K9.8):**
  - **The effective level:** `CrawlerSkills.Level(skills, worn, work)` is the one number every effect and choice reads.
  - **Practice's one entry:** `ISkillPractice.Practise(agent, work, experience, source)`, implemented by `SimulationManager.Practise`. It logs a level gained and tells a band reached in the chronicle (`SkillBandReached`).
  - **Sources:** `PracticeSource` is a kind and a ceiling, and the only kind today is `Work`.
- **Practice's four callers:**
  - the fight (`CreatureService.Rule.Land`: a hit or a shot);
  - the harvest (`HarvestResourceIntentHandler`);
  - the stations (`WorkshopService`, a make done);
  - the rescue (`CarryIntentHandler`).
- **The checks:**
  - `SkillsValidator`;
  - `SkillNamesTests`: code names a work, never a skill;
  - `SkillReadsTests`: learned levels are read only in the seam, practice's entry and the dossier's list;
  - `SkillProofTests`: Mycology, a skill added as data;
  - `PracticeMakesAVeteranTests.MeasurePracticeOnTheGateSeeds`: explicit, seven days.
- **Cognition:** `AgentContext.Skills` is a copy. The salvage choice scores by price × mining speed × `SkillSpeed` / distance (`ResourceTargeting`). The planner holds 46 of 64 facts.
- **Economy:**
  - **The guild:** a member's wage is at least its reservation wage (5g × its greed factor; default 5g, at most 50g) per caravan visit. Paid: +2 trust and +1 warmth. Unpaid: −10 trust and −5 warmth. A member quits under trust 30 or after two unpaid visits in a row (`GuildTuning`, `GuildService.PayWages`).
  - **Artisans:** their contracts mirror a member's (`WorkshopService.PayWages`).
- **Saves:**
  - Save format 11. `CrawlerRecord` ends with `Skills`.
  - The stall's goods table holds 24 (`TradeStallData.GoodsCapacity`).
- **Open, and pressed by S2:** the whole loop's economy (Epic 10 Part 2's F14, joined by Part 3's F14, F8 and F10; `docs/economy-model.md` §8). Upkeep adds a cost to a loop that is already strained, so S2.4 measures it beside those findings and the producer sets the bands.
  - **The producer's word (2026-11-16, with the go):** "Economy flukes are tolerable until they completely ruin our testing scenarios." So nothing in this plan retunes the economy for its own sake. A scenario or band broken by an economy fluke is judged across seeds first. The loop is retuned only if no seed choice saves the scenario, and then it is flagged to the producer.

## 1. Decisions proposed (defaults; the producer answers by exception)
- **D1, the traits record** (A3): `CrawlerTraits` lives in `Simulation/Skills/`, because cognition reads it and may read nothing of `People/`. It is a fixed, unmanaged record of 256 bytes, its size pinned, in `CrawlerRecord` after `Skills`. Its sections:
  - **Passions:** 16 bytes, one per skill id (S1).
  - **Techniques:** 48 bytes, three bands for each of 16 skills, reserved for plan 2.
  - **Teachers:** four slots, each a skill and the teacher's `EntityId` (16 bytes), 80 bytes in all. They hold the first teacher of each of the first four skills a crawler is taught (S3). Four, not sixteen: an id is 16 bytes, and a crawler is taught in a few skills at most.
  - **Deeds:** 16 counters of 2 bytes each (S0.5).
  - **Later fields:** a calling, an epithet, four earned traits and four scars, reserved for plans 2 and 3.
  - **Spare:** the rest, up to 256 bytes.
- **D2, deeds are a table** (A2, A7): a `Deed` id under 16, one row each in `DeedTable` (code name, the player's word).
  - **Counted:** where they happen, on the main thread. A creature slain (the blow that killed it), a boss slain, a rescue, a make, a downing in danger survived, a hunt paid, a clear won, a crawler taught (S3).
  - **Never rebuilt from the chronicle.** First blood is a count of one, derived, not stored.
  - **Why now:** counted from the start, the deeds give S0's measurement its data, and S6's epithets read a real history.
- **D3, passions are a table and a draw** (A2, Q7):
  - **The table:** `PassionTable` has three rows: none, keen, burning. Each row gives the word, the practice multiplier (×1, ×1.5, ×2), the frustration eased by each act of practice (0, 2, 4) and the glyph.
  - **The draw:** at spawn, after the skills' draw, on a new `SimRandomSalt.Passions` (17), so no earlier draw moves (looks, names, skills).
  - **The odds:** no keen passion 40%, one 40%, two 20%, and a burning one 25%. Each is picked among the skills at odds of the row's lean (as talents are) and doubled for a talent. A skill holds one passion at most. Drifters draw passions too.
- **D4, a passion speeds every source of practice:** the multiplier applies in practice's one entry, before the ceiling, to the work, a mentor, a manual and the yard alike. "No passion" is today's rate, so K9's numbers stand for the passionless (§2.2).
- **D5, practice by challenge** (Q8):
  - **The source carries a challenge:** `PracticeSource` gains a `Challenge`, the level the act demands.
  - **The factor:** practice is scaled by a table in `SkillRules`, by how far the crawler's level stands above the challenge. At or below it, ×1. Then, one level at a time: ×0.75, ×0.55, ×0.4, ×0.25, ×0.2, ×0.15, and ×0.1 at seven or more.
  - **What each act demands:**
    - a creature kind's `Challenge` is a new column in `CreatureCatalog`: rats 2, spinners 5, beetles 7, the Matriarch 9, the Gatekeeper 10;
    - a harvest's comes from the item's `HarvestChallenge` (a new field: herbs 3, ore 4, glowstone 7). An Apprentice learns fully from the common seams, and a Journeyman has to seek the Gallery's glowstone;
    - a make's comes from its recipe's `Challenge` (a new column in `RecipeBook`: a pickaxe or a draught 3, arms and armour 5–7, set at S1.3), and a repair is 2;
    - a rescue is always 10.
  - So a rat teaches a Novice fully and a Journeyman 6 a quarter, and a beetle teaches a Journeyman fully (Q8's examples).
- **D6, passions weigh the work they love** (A10):
  - **Where:** the salvage choice multiplies a node's score by the passion's weight for the harvested item's skill (keen ×1.15, burning ×1.3), through `Considerations`.
  - **What it costs:** no planner fact.
  - **Not yet:** the fight's choices and the guild's orders don't read passions in this plan.
- **D7, the mastery budget** (Q6):
  - **The rule:** `SkillRules.BudgetCeiling(skills, skill)` gives 10, 8 or 5. A skill stops at 5 while two others stand past Apprentice (6 or more), and at 8 while another stands at Master (9 or more).
  - **Who gets a slot:** first come, first served, in the order practice arrives.
  - **Where it applies:** practice's one entry, together with the source's ceiling. Nobody gives a skill up (Q6).
  - **Starting skills:** a starting talent is at most 5, so no draw breaks the budget.
- **D8, the asking wage** (Q9):
  - **How much:** a member's or an artisan's asking wage is its reservation wage times its best band's raise: ×1 to Apprentice, ×1.25 for a Journeyman, ×1.5 for a Master. The raises are numbers in `GuildTuning`, and the artisans read the same rows.
  - **A raise unpaid:** at a caravan visit, a crawler paid in full but below its asking wage takes the unpaid wage's trust and warmth (Q9: "as an unpaid wage does"). No unpaid visit is counted, so it quits only when its trust falls under the line.
  - **The ask is told:** the chronicle tells it once per band reached ("asks for a raise").
  - **The ledger:** wages are transfers, so it is unchanged.
- **D9, mentors** (Q10):
  - **Who teaches:** when a crawler practises a work, a better hand teaches it, at source `Mentor`. The better hand's effective level is at least three above the learner's, it practised the same work within the last game hour, and it stands within 6 units.
  - **How much:** half the act's experience, up to the mentor's level less two. A passion multiplies it as well.
  - **Lineage:** the learner records its first teacher in the skill (D1's slots), and the mentor counts a "taught" deed.
  - **Not saved:** each crawler's last practice (work and tick) is work in progress, so after a load nobody mentors until they practise again.
- **D10, the drill yard** (Q10):
  - **The station:** a station of a new kind, `DrillYard` (3), in the guildhall. Built through the existing `BuildStationCommand` for 200g (the `Construction` sink). It has no recipes.
  - **Who drills:** a guild member with no orders, fed and rested, drills there in free hours. It practises the fight work of the weapon it wears (melee when unarmed), at source `Yard`: 30 experience a game hour, up to 5.
  - **Paid for by the building:** building the yard is the patron's payment for teaching. Nothing is charged per hour.
- **D11, manuals** (Q10):
  - **The items:** one manual per skill. Each is an item row with a `ManualBlock`: the work it teaches, 300 experience in all, and its ceiling. Plan 1 ships primers, teaching to 3, at 25g.
  - **"3 or 5":** Q10's other ceiling is the same block's ceiling, so a treatise teaching to 5 is one row more whenever the producer wants it.
  - **Bought and read:** the caravan brings them, the stall sells them, and a crawler buys one for a skill below the manual's ceiling, its passions first. It reads over two game hours, in rest, at its bunk or a hearth, and the manual is used up.
  - **The goods table** grows from 24 to 32 inside format 12, the PR's one raise.
- **D12, practice is observed, never stored for decisions:** `SimulationManager.Practised` (the agent, the skill, the experience granted and its source) is raised from the one entry for observers (measurements, DoDs, `OutpostWatch`). No rule reads it.

## 2. The milestones, phased

### S0. Groundwork (2–3 days)
**Complexity:** Possibly Complex: saved state (format 12) and a measurement. No behaviour change, and golden moves only for the hash's new coverage, if at all.
- [ ] **S0.1 The hand-over checked, and the skills model** [Basic: docs from the code as it stands]

  - Create `docs/skills-model.md` with what stands today: the levels and bands, what each act is worth, what a level does, the draw, the seam and practice's entry. Link it from the epic and from AGENTS.md's skills paragraph.
- [ ] **S0.2 Practice observed** [Basic: an event; no behaviour change]
  - D12's `Practised` event, raised after the ceiling.
  - `OutpostWatch` tallies practice by skill and by kind of source.
  - Golden unchanged.
- [ ] **S0.3 The measurement** [Possibly Complex: measuring]
  - **The test:** `MeasureGrowthOnTheGateSeeds`, explicit, over the 23 gate seeds and 14 game days. Two runs: no player, and the `hunting` profile's players.
  - **Per seed:**
    - levels and bands gained, per skill, per resident;
    - who holds two or more skills past Apprentice by day 14 (the budget's pressure);

  - **The results** go to `docs/skills-model.md`. A surprise becomes a finding, numbered F1 onward.
  - **The question it answers:** whether D7's budget ever binds in 14 days. If not, S2's DoD needs a longer run or a seed where it does.
- [ ] **S0.4 The traits record** [Possibly Complex: saved state, format 12]
  - **The record:** D1's `CrawlerTraits`, as `AgentState.Traits`, written and read in `CaptureRecord` and `RestoreRecord`, and copied to cognition as `AgentContext.Traits`, as `Skills` is.
  - **The format:** `SessionSaveHeader.CurrentFormatVersion` 12, the PR's one raise. The record's size is pinned beside the economy records' (`EconomySerializationValidator`).
  - **The round trip** in `SessionCaptureValidator` and `SessionRestoreValidator`.
  - **The world hash** mixes the record only when it isn't empty, so golden doesn't move in this step.
  - **Old saves:** a format 11 save is refused with its reason.
- [ ] **S0.5 Deeds** [Possibly Complex: call sites across the core, saved]
  - **The table:** D2's `Deed` and `DeedTable`, counted in the record.
  - **The counts,** each where its deed happens:
    - the creatures' kill: the slayer, and the boss's;
    - the carry's rescue;
    - the station's make;
    - standing up from a downing in danger;
    - the hunt's payout: each hunter paid;
    - the clear's delivery: each member home.
  - **The checks:**
    - a validator per deed: counted once, saved, and loaded;
    - `DeedNamesTests`: production code names a deed only in its table and where it is counted.
  - **Golden:** if the hash moves (a deed before t3000 on a golden seed), it is re-recorded in its own commit, named as the hash's new coverage.
- [ ] **S0.6 S0's gate and docs** [Basic]
  - **The gate:** the index's gates.
  - **Docs:** the epic's §0 (D1, D2, D12) and §4 (S0); AGENTS.md's crawler record (the traits record beside the skills) and "Adding a deed".

### S1. Passions and challenge (3–4 days), then the producer's look

  - **The checks:**

- [ ] **S1.3 Practice by challenge** [Possibly Complex: a behaviour change, numbers for each kind]
  - **The source:** `PracticeSource.Challenge`, and D5's table.
  - **The columns:** creatures, items and recipes.
  - **The callers:** the four callers pass the challenge of what they practise on.
  - **The checks:**
    - `SkillsValidator` repeats Q8's examples: a rat teaches a Novice fully and a Journeyman a quarter, and a beetle teaches a Journeyman fully;

- [ ] **S1.4 Passions weigh the work** [Possibly Complex: cognition reads the copy]
