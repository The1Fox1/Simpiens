- Track A: Core Simulation & Cognition Engine (Original Linear Sequence: Epics 1-11)
- Track B: UI / UX & Player Interface (Branching Track: Epic UI-X)
- Track C: Harness, Tooling & Diagnostics (Branching Track: Epic Test-X)
